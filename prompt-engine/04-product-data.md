# PRODUCT DATA

## Purpose

This document defines how product information should be interpreted by the Prompt Engine.

The product is the center of the commercial narrative.

Every scene, interaction and camera movement should reinforce the product's value.

The Prompt Engine must transform raw product information into a compelling visual story.

---

Every product should contain the following information.

## Basic Information

Product Name

Category

Model

Color

Dimensions

Weight

---

## Commercial Information

Main Benefit

---

## Product State

New

Opened

Being Used

In Action

Before Use

After Use

Unboxing

Lifestyle

The selected state determines how the product will appear in the video.

---

# Product Understanding

The Prompt Engine should understand the product before creating the prompt.

Ask internally:

What problem does this product solve?

Why would someone buy it?

How should the audience emotionally react?

What is the strongest visual feature?

What should receive the most screen time?

---

# Product Priority

Always prioritize showing:

1. Main Benefit

2. Product Usage

3. Product Design

4. Product Details

5. Brand Identity

The audience must understand the value before the CTA.

---

# Product Visibility

The product should never disappear for long periods.

Whenever possible:

Keep it visible.

Keep it in focus.

Use it naturally.

Highlight important details.

Avoid unnecessary occlusion.

---

# Product Interaction

The avatar should interact naturally with the product.

Examples

Holding

Opening

Touching

Using

Rotating

Showing Details

Demonstrating Features

Comparing

Inspecting

Presenting

Every interaction must have a purpose.

---

# Hero Moments

Every commercial should include at least one Hero Shot.

A Hero Shot is the moment where the product becomes the visual focus.

Possible Hero Shots

Front View

45° Angle

Macro Detail

Slow Rotation

Hand Presentation

Packaging Close-up

Luxury Reveal

Beauty Shot

The Hero Shot should feel premium.

The audience should immediately understand:

How it works.

How to hold it.

How to use it.

Why it is useful.

What problem it solves.

Never rely only on narration.

Show visually.

# Emotional Value

Products are not purchased only for functional reasons.

The Prompt Engine should communicate emotional value.

Examples

Confidence

Comfort

Convenience

Luxury

Health

Beauty

Security

Productivity

Status

Fun

The emotional value should appear visually.

---

# Commercial Priority

The video should answer these questions naturally:

What is it?

Why is it useful?

Why is it different?

Why should I trust it?

Why should I buy it?

---

# Common Mistakes

Avoid

Showing the product too late.

Keeping the product out of frame.

Unnatural interaction.

Incorrect proportions.

Floating products.

Low visibility.

Wrong scale.

Brand distortion.

Logo distortion.

Packaging changes.

---

# Final Rule

The product is the hero of the commercial.

Every camera movement, scene transition, interaction and composition should increase the perceived value of the product.

The viewer should finish the video knowing exactly:

What the product is.

How it works.

Why it is valuable.

Why they should want it.
