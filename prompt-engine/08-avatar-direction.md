# AVATAR DIRECTION

## Purpose

This document defines how the Prompt Engine should direct the avatar throughout the generated video.

The avatar is not a fictional character.

The avatar is a real person represented by the attached reference image.

The objective is to preserve identity while directing realistic human behavior.

The Prompt Engine should behave like a professional film director.

---

# Core Philosophy

The avatar is the face of the commercial.

Every movement...

Every expression...

Every look...

Every gesture...

Must feel completely natural.

The audience should believe the avatar is a real creator recording a real video.

Never create robotic behavior.

Never create exaggerated acting.

Authenticity always wins.

---

# Identity Preservation

The avatar reference image is the single source of truth.

Always preserve:

• Face

• Skin tone

• Hair

• Hairstyle

• Hair color

• Eyes

• Eyebrows

• Lips

• Nose

• Jawline

• Body proportions

• Age appearance

• Clothing

• Accessories

Never modify identity.

Never replace identity.

Never generate another person.

---

# Human Behavior

Every action should resemble real human behavior.

Avoid perfectly symmetrical movements.

Allow natural imperfections.

Small pauses.

Micro expressions.

Natural breathing.

Natural blinking.

Weight shifting.

Comfortable posture.

The avatar should never feel animated.

---

# Facial Expressions

Expressions must always match the story.

Possible emotions:

Neutral

Happy

Excited

Curious

Confident

Surprised

Relieved

Focused

Satisfied

Inspired

Calm

Friendly

Authentic

Luxury

Professional

Avoid exaggerated emotions.

The face should communicate naturally.

---

# Eye Direction

Eyes communicate attention.

Possible directions:

Looking directly into the camera

Looking at the product

Looking at hands

Looking around naturally

Looking toward another object

Eye movement should always feel motivated.

Avoid frozen eyes.

---

# Body Language

Body posture should reinforce confidence.

Use:

Relaxed shoulders

Natural standing position

Balanced posture

Comfortable walking

Controlled gestures

Confident movement

Avoid:

Rigid posture

Mechanical movement

Overacting

Unnatural poses

---

# Hand Interaction

Hands are extremely important.

The avatar may:

Hold the product

Rotate the product

Open packaging

Touch buttons

Point to details

Use the product

Present the product

Support the product

Every hand movement should have purpose.

Avoid random gestures.

---

# Walking

If walking is present:

Walk naturally.

Maintain realistic speed.

Relaxed posture.

Natural arm swing.

No exaggerated movement.

---

# Sitting

If seated:

Maintain natural posture.

Interact comfortably.

Avoid stiffness.

Use realistic weight distribution.

---

# Speaking

If dialogue exists:

The avatar should appear conversational.

Never look like a television presenter.

Speak naturally.

Use realistic pauses.

Use authentic facial movement.

---

# Smile

Smiles should match the context.

Natural smile

Soft smile

Confident smile

Excited smile

Subtle smile

Avoid artificial permanent smiles.

---

# Product Interaction

The avatar should always treat the product naturally.

Possible interactions:

Inspect

Use

Hold

Carry

Rotate

Demonstrate

Present

Compare

React

The product should feel like part of the avatar's daily life.

---

# Environment Interaction

The avatar should naturally interact with the environment.

Examples:

Walk

Sit

Lean

Open doors

Use furniture

Pick up objects

Avoid floating behavior.

Avoid ignoring the environment.

---

# Emotional Progression

Emotion should evolve.

Curiosity

↓

Interest

↓

Confidence

↓

Satisfaction

↓

Recommendation

Avoid emotional stagnation.

---

# TikTok Behavior

The avatar should resemble a real TikTok creator.

Characteristics:

Authentic

Friendly

Natural

Energetic

Approachable

Trustworthy

Never resemble a television commercial actor.

---

# Luxury Behavior

When using Cinematic style:

Reduce movement.

Increase elegance.

Increase confidence.

Use slower gestures.

Premium posture.

Controlled expressions.

Luxury behavior should feel effortless.

---

# UGC Behavior

When using UGC:

More expressive.

Natural mistakes.

Conversational gestures.

Relaxed posture.

Handheld energy.

Authenticity over perfection.

---

# POV Behavior

When using POV:

The avatar appears through:

Hands

Reflections

Shadows

Mirror shots

Partial body

Avoid constantly showing the full face.

The audience should feel immersed.

---

# Consistency

The avatar must remain visually and emotionally consistent.

Do not suddenly change:

Mood

Energy

Confidence

Posture

Expressions

Body language

Identity

Everything should feel like the same person from beginning to end.

---

# Common Mistakes

Avoid:

Robotic movement

Frozen expressions

Mechanical blinking

Perfect symmetry

Artificial smiles

Overacting

Fake reactions

Emotionless face

Stiff posture

Exaggerated gestures

Identity drift

Unrealistic movement

---

# Avatar Priority

When directing a scene:

1. Preserve identity

2. Preserve realism

3. Preserve emotion

4. Preserve product interaction

5. Preserve story

Everything else is secondary.

---

# Final Rule

The avatar should feel like a real person, not an AI-generated character.

Every movement should communicate authenticity.

Every expression should reinforce trust.

The audience should connect emotionally with the avatar before connecting with the product.

The avatar is not acting.

The avatar is living the moment.
