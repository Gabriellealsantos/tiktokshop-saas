# PLATFORM

## Purpose

This document defines the platform-specific rules that every generated prompt must follow.

The target platform is TikTok Shop.

Every generated video should feel native to TikTok.

The objective is not to create a traditional commercial.

The objective is to create content that naturally integrates into the TikTok For You feed while maximizing product conversions.

---

# Platform

Target Platform

TikTok Shop

Primary Goal

Drive product sales through authentic short-form video content.

---

# Core Philosophy

Every generated video should feel like it was recorded by a real creator.

Never create content that feels like:

• Television Commercial

• Corporate Advertisement

• Promotional Infomercial

• Traditional Brand Commercial

Instead create content that feels:

Native

Authentic

Personal

Natural

Fast

Engaging

Trustworthy

Organic

---

# Video Format

Aspect Ratio

9:16

Orientation

Vertical

Fullscreen

Safe Area

Keep the product and avatar inside the central safe zone.

Avoid important visual elements close to screen borders.

---

# Video Duration

Preferred

15–30 seconds

Shorter videos may be used for aggressive conversion.

Longer videos should only be generated when storytelling requires it.

Every second should have purpose.

---

# Hook

The first three seconds determine whether the viewer continues watching.

Always begin with:

Movement

Curiosity

Unexpected action

Strong product interaction

Pattern interrupt

Visual intrigue

Never begin slowly.

Never waste the opening.

---

# Pacing

TikTok requires fast visual progression.

Each scene should introduce:

New information

New movement

New emotion

New product feature

Avoid static shots lasting too long.

Maintain constant visual interest.

---

# Product Visibility

The product should appear early.

The audience should immediately understand:

What is being advertised.

Why it matters.

How it is used.

The product should remain visible throughout the commercial.

---

# Avatar Behavior

The avatar should resemble a successful TikTok creator.

Characteristics:

Friendly

Authentic

Natural

Confident

Expressive

Conversational

Avoid television presenter behavior.

Avoid scripted acting.

---

# Camera Language

Preferred:

Handheld

Eye level

Natural movement

Medium close-up

Close-up

POV

Occasional macro

The camera should feel human.

Avoid excessive cinematic complexity unless Cinematic style is selected.

---

# Editing Style

Fast.

Dynamic.

Fluid.

Natural.

Motivated.

Every cut should increase engagement.

Avoid slow commercial pacing.

---

# Storytelling

Stories should feel personal.

Recommended structures:

Problem → Solution

Before → After

Personal Experience

Unexpected Discovery

Product Test

Lifestyle

Review

Comparison

Avoid complex narratives.

Keep the story immediately understandable.

---

# Emotional Goal

Create:

Curiosity

Trust

Desire

Authenticity

Confidence

Satisfaction

The viewer should feel emotionally connected before receiving the CTA.

---

# Product Demonstration

Always demonstrate visually.

Never rely only on dialogue.

The audience should understand:

How it works.

How it feels.

Why it is useful.

The demonstration should happen naturally.

---

# Environment

Use realistic environments.

Every location should feel believable.

The environment should support the lifestyle associated with the product.

Avoid unrealistic studio environments unless explicitly requested.

---

# Audio Awareness

Even if no audio is generated, the visual pacing should suggest:

Natural conversation

Authentic reactions

Real-world ambience

Organic rhythm

Avoid exaggerated lip movements.

Avoid theatrical acting.

---

# Call To Action

The CTA should feel earned.

Never interrupt the story.

Possible CTAs:

Check it out.

See the product.

Tap the product.

Discover more.

Get yours.

The CTA should feel natural rather than aggressive.

---

# Commercial Authenticity

The audience should believe:

The avatar genuinely uses the product.

The reaction is authentic.

The recommendation is honest.

The product fits naturally into everyday life.

Trust is more valuable than exaggerated selling.

---

# TikTok Algorithm Mindset

Every generated video should optimize for:

Watch Time

Retention

Completion Rate

Replays

Shares

Comments

Saves

Purchase Intent

Every scene should encourage the viewer to continue watching.

---

# Common Mistakes

Avoid:

Television commercials.

Corporate language.

Long introductions.

Product appearing too late.

Slow pacing.

Artificial dialogue.

Static framing.

Overly polished acting.

Forced emotional reactions.

Excessive visual effects.

Everything should feel native to TikTok.

---

# Final Rule

The viewer should never feel they are watching an advertisement.

They should feel they are watching a creator naturally sharing a product they genuinely enjoy using.

The best TikTok Shop videos don't feel like ads.

They feel like authentic recommendations.

Every generated prompt should reflect this philosophy.
