# PRODUCT DIRECTION

## Purpose

This document defines how the Prompt Engine should direct, present and emphasize the product throughout the generated video.

The product is the commercial hero.

Every camera movement, composition, interaction and lighting decision should increase the perceived value of the product.

The Prompt Engine should think like a professional advertising director.

The objective is not simply to show the product.

The objective is to make the audience desire the product.

---

# Commercial Philosophy

Products should never feel like objects.

Products should feel desirable.

Every appearance of the product should increase:

• curiosity

• trust

• perceived quality

• emotional value

• purchase desire

Every shot must answer:

"Why would someone want this product?"

---

# Product Priority

The product is always the visual hero.

Priority:

1. Product

2. Avatar interaction

3. Product details

4. Environment

Everything else supports the product.

---

# Product Integrity

Always preserve the reference image.

Never modify:

• shape

• proportions

• dimensions

• colors

• materials

• textures

• packaging

• logo

• labels

• branding

• accessories

The product must remain visually identical to the reference image.

---

# Product Visibility

The product should never disappear for long periods.

Maintain frequent visual contact.

The audience should always know:

Where the product is.

How it is being used.

What feature is being demonstrated.

Avoid:

Hidden product

Blurred product

Obstructed product

Incorrect scale

Partial visibility

---

# Product Introduction

The first appearance of the product should feel intentional.

Possible introductions:

Natural reveal

Hand reveal

Table reveal

Pocket reveal

Bag reveal

Close-up reveal

Hero reveal

Lifestyle reveal

The reveal should create curiosity.

---

# Hero Shot

Every commercial must contain at least one Hero Shot.

Characteristics

Perfect composition.

Premium lighting.

Clean background.

Controlled movement.

Maximum product visibility.

Luxury feeling.

The Hero Shot should become the visual climax of the commercial.

# Macro Details

Whenever appropriate, highlight:

Texture

Buttons

Finish

Materials

Packaging

Display

Premium craftsmanship

Macro shots increase perceived quality.

---

# Product Rotation

When rotating the product:

Move slowly.

Maintain stability.

Keep branding visible.

Avoid unrealistic spinning.

The movement should feel premium.

---

# Product Scale

Maintain realistic proportions.

The product should never appear:

Too large.

Too small.

Floating.

Distorted.

Incorrectly scaled.

Realistic scale reinforces credibility.

---

# Product Movement

Only move the product when there is purpose.

Possible movements:

Lift

Rotate

Slide

Open

Activate

Use

Present

Every movement should communicate value.

---

# Product Timing

Do not wait too long to show the product.

For conversion videos:

The product should appear within the first few seconds.

The audience should immediately understand what is being advertised.

---

# Product Composition

Whenever possible:

Center attention on the product.

Use negative space.

Create depth.

Guide the eye.

Avoid visual clutter.

The product should always be easy to identify.

---

# Lighting

Lighting should enhance:

Materials

Texture

Premium finish

Shape

Reflections

Details

Avoid lighting that hides important features.

# Common Mistakes

Avoid

Product hidden.

Incorrect colors.

Wrong logo.

Changing packaging.

Wrong proportions.

Artificial reflections.

Floating product.

Unrealistic interaction.

Too much focus on the avatar.

Ignoring important features.

The product must always remain believable.

---

# Final Rule

The product is the protagonist.

The avatar introduces it.

The environment supports it.

The camera celebrates it.

Every scene should increase the perceived value of the product.

By the end of the video, the audience should clearly understand:

What the product is.

How it works.

Why it is valuable.

Why they should want it.
