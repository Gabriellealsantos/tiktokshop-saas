# VIDEO OBJECTIVES

## Purpose

This document defines the commercial objective of the generated video.

Every generated prompt must be built around a single primary objective.

The objective influences:

- Storytelling
- Camera language
- Pacing
- Product interaction
- Emotional progression
- Call-to-action
- Scene composition

Never generate a video without a clearly defined objective.

---

# Objective Selection

Every generation must have ONE primary objective.

Possible objectives:

• Conversion

• Awareness

• Engagement

• Education

• Social Proof

• Product Demonstration

• Brand Authority

• Lead Generation

• Community Building

---

# 1. Conversion

Goal

Maximize purchases.

The viewer should immediately understand why they should buy the product.

Characteristics

• Strong hook

• Product appears early

• Product demonstrated immediately

• Emotional desire

• Clear value proposition

• Clear CTA

Focus

"What will I gain if I buy this?"

Ideal for

TikTok Shop

E-commerce

Affiliate marketing

---

# 2. Awareness

Goal

Introduce the product.

The objective is not immediate purchase.

Instead, create curiosity.

Characteristics

• Product reveal

• Lifestyle

• Emotional connection

• Brand introduction

• Soft CTA

Focus

"What is this product?"

---

# 3. Engagement

Goal

Increase comments, likes and shares.

Characteristics

Conversation starters

Questions

Unexpected moments

Humor

Curiosity

Open loops

Focus

"I need to watch until the end."

---

# 4. Education

Goal

Teach something valuable.

Characteristics

Explain

Show

Compare

Demonstrate

Educate

The product naturally becomes the solution.

Focus

"Now I understand."

---

# 5. Social Proof

Goal

Increase trust.

Characteristics

Real reactions

Authentic emotions

Before and after

Testimonials

Natural behavior

Lifestyle

Focus

"People actually use this."

---

# 6. Product Demonstration

Goal

Show how the product works.

Characteristics

Close-ups

Macro shots

Step-by-step

Hands-on interaction

Feature highlights

Problem solving

Focus

"I can clearly see how it works."

---

# 7. Brand Authority

Goal

Increase perceived quality.

Characteristics

Premium cinematography

Elegant movements

Luxury lighting

High-end environments

Confident avatar

Focus

"This brand looks premium."

---

# 8. Lead Generation

Goal

Encourage the next action.

Characteristics

Curiosity

Value

Trust

Simple CTA

No aggressive selling

Focus

"I want to know more."

---

# 9. Community Building

Goal

Create belonging.

Characteristics

Natural conversations

Relatable situations

Shared experiences

Authenticity

Lifestyle

Focus

"This creator is just like me."

# Objective Priority

If multiple objectives are requested, always prioritize:

1. Conversion

2. Product Demonstration

3. Social Proof

4. Awareness

5. Engagement

6. Education

7. Brand Authority

8. Lead Generation

9. Community Building

Only one primary objective should guide the final prompt.

---

# Final Rule

Every scene, every camera movement, every facial expression and every product interaction must reinforce the selected objective.

Nothing should appear in the video without supporting that objective.
