# STORY STRUCTURE

## Purpose

This document defines how the Prompt Engine should build compelling narratives for short-form videos.

Every generated video must tell a story.

The objective is not simply to present a product.

The objective is to create curiosity, maintain attention, generate emotion and motivate action.

Every story must feel natural, visually engaging and optimized for TikTok.

---

# Core Principles

Every story should answer four questions:

Why should I keep watching?

Why should I care?

Why should I trust this?

Why should I buy this?

Every scene must contribute to at least one of these questions.

---

# Story Flow

Every video follows a narrative progression.

Attention

↓

Interest

↓

Curiosity

↓

Value

↓

Emotion

↓

Action

The viewer should never feel that scenes are random.

Every scene should naturally lead to the next one.

---

# Story Components

A complete story contains:

Hook

Context

Problem

Solution

Demonstration

Transformation

Proof

Call To Action

Not every structure uses every component, but every video must feel complete.

# Emotional Progression

Every story should evolve emotionally.

Curiosity

↓

Interest

↓

Confidence

↓

Desire

↓

Action

Avoid emotional flatness.

---

# Hook Rules

The first three seconds are critical.

Possible hooks

Unexpected statement.

Visual surprise.

Strong movement.

Problem.

Question.

Comparison.

Transformation.

Product reveal.

The hook should immediately create curiosity.

---

# Demonstration Rules

Always demonstrate.

Never only describe.

The audience should SEE the value.

Avoid relying only on dialogue.

Use visuals first.

---

# Product Timing

Do not wait too long.

The product should appear naturally within the first moments whenever the objective is conversion.

---

# Story Rhythm

Fast.

Fluid.

Purposeful.

Every scene should move the story forward.

Avoid repetitive scenes.

Avoid unnecessary pauses.

---

# Scene Progression

Each scene should introduce something new.

New information.

New movement.

New emotion.

New product feature.

Never repeat the same visual idea.

---

# Call To Action

The CTA should feel earned.

Never interrupt the story.

The CTA should conclude the narrative naturally.

---

# Common Mistakes

Avoid

No hook.

Weak opening.

Random scenes.

Product appears too late.

No emotional progression.

Over-explaining.

Artificial dialogue.

Long static shots.

Repetitive demonstrations.

Forced CTA.

---

# Final Rule

Every generated video must feel like a complete story.

The audience should experience curiosity, emotion and value before reaching the final call-to-action.

A successful commercial is not a sequence of scenes.

It is a narrative that naturally leads the viewer toward action.
